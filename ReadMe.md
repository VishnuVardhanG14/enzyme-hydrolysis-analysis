## Enzyme Hydrolysis Data Analysis

A self mini project for analysing a synthetically generated enzyme hydrolysis data set and get familiarized with R programming.

###Objective: 
- Evaluate the effect of temperature and hydrolysis time on biocompound yield

- Identify statistical relationship and significant difference between process parameters

- Visualize relationship between experimental conditions and biocompound yield

###Research questions:

-Does temperature have an impact on the protein and sugar production?

-Similarly, does hydrolysis time effect the biocompound yield?

-What is the relationship between these parameters and the concentration of protein and sugar?

-Based on the analysis, what is the most favorable condition to get the maximum biocompound concentration?

###Dataset

The dataset was synthetically generated. It may not represent real experimental results and is intended for practise

###Tools and packages used
- R program
- Quarto
- Tidyverse
- ggplot

###Statistical methods
- Correlation
- ANOVA

###Key findings
- The protein and reducing sugar concentration increased linearly with increase in incubation time and temperature

- Temperature had very less influence on protein concentration while it played a significant role for reducing sugar

- Wherever protein increased, the reducing sugar also tend to increase. However, this does not show that both affect each other

###Limitations
- This is a self generated dataset and may not represent real experimental data
- The limited experimental conditions and small sample size may reduce accuracy
- Limited statistical analysis may reduce reliability
- The results are not experimentally validated and may not be reproduced in real life
